# 0.11.4 provenance

Kael shock fronts and biome debris are original procedural rendering using the project's existing painted slam frames. Critical impact crowns, pop animation and six rarity materials are original code-native graphics, reusing the project's existing slate atlas. No third-party artwork was copied. Previous starter sounds remain unchanged, including their original backups and revert instructions.

Patch artwork reuses the original Hollow Harvest cover and game icons. Cinzel and Source Sans 3 licenses accompany release archives. The project uses extensive AI assistance in programming, original art, music and sound, under human creative direction and playtesting.
