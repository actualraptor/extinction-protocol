# Extinction Protocol 0.9.0 — Lasting Expeditions

## Bosses and portals
- Defeated bosses no longer leave queued spit attacks, ground attacks or broods behind.
- Portals wait until the current reward has been claimed.
- Completed runs cannot receive late damage or score changes.

## Daily and rewards
- Caches and shrines wait while another reward is open.
- New Daily circuits refresh exploration and breakable objects.
- Daily rewards cannot be rerolled.
- Daily rankings compare attempts from the current ruleset; older records remain saved.

## Progression
- Damaged progress files can recover from a safety copy.
- Unlocks, research, amber and records carry forward from earlier versions.
- If progress cannot be recovered, the game protects the existing files instead of overwriting them.

## Interface
- All five survivors fit on smaller character-selection screens.
- Long button captions no longer push neighbouring controls off-screen.
- Large scores, amber totals, timers and Daily circuit labels stay within their frames.
- Patch-history headings stay inside their header.
- Updated the field manual's Daily description.
