# Hollow Harvest assets

The haunted menu and seasonal character/creature overlays are original generated artwork. Prompts and tool provenance are recorded in `assets/PROVENANCE-HALLOWEEN-MENU.md` and `assets/PROVENANCE-HOLLOW-HARVEST.md`.

The nine seasonal soundtrack loops and pumpkin-club effect were synthesized for this game. Their reproducible source is `tools/build_hollow_harvest_music.py`. No music from Solstice, Vampire Survivors or Megabonk is included.

Kael's eighteen voice recordings were supplied by the project owner and copied into event-specific filenames. The original recordings are retained separately in the workspace. Voice playback uses an independent random source and never changes gameplay rewards.

Earlier art, sound and font provenance remains in PROVENANCE.md and the bundled font licenses. This hobby project uses extensive AI assistance in code, artwork, music and sound, with human direction and playtesting.
