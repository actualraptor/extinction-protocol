# 0.14.4 — Meteor Rework

Meteor's reconstructed model and redesigned encounter are now included in the public Windows build.

- Animated molten core, recessed glowing eyes, articulated rock shell and orbiting debris.
- Burning entrance across the screen, followed by a return dive and ground impact. The encounter clock starts after landing.
- Each three-wave rubble volley holds one safe sector; the next volley chooses a different direction.
- Advancing fire replaces the arena boundary and countdown display. The 210-second deadline remains, with rapidly increasing damage after time runs out.
- Updated impact and attack sound effects. Gameplay audio is enabled.
- T-rex and Triceratops reworks remain included.

Windows release performance was checked at 1080p with 400 enemies and late-fight fire on an RTX 5070 Ti: average frame time 10.2 ms; 95th percentile 13.6 ms. Results will vary across hardware. Meteor presentation and balance will continue to receive updates.

Extract the ZIP and launch **Extinction Protocol.exe**. Existing saves are retained.
