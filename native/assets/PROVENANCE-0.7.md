# Artwork and audio provenance — Frontiers 0.7

Original generated raster art, made for this project with the image generation tool:
- frontiers-07.png: illustrated weapons, local creatures and environment props.
- iona-walk.png and orin-walk.png: directional walking poses.
- frontier-ground-07.png: six ground materials.

Alpha bounds are indexed by tools/index_frontiers.py into JSON; the generated PNGs remain unedited. Existing assets retain their previous provenance records.

Original synthesized music and weapon effects are authored in tools/compose_frontiers.py. Nine compositions and their durations are listed in audio/frontiers-score.json. Solstice NES informed the layered chip instrumentation and contrasting composition sections. No existing score, recording, melody or sample was imported.
