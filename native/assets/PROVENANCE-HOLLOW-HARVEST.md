# Hollow Harvest assets

Created for Extinction Protocol with the built-in image generation tool; generated
from the project's original self-made AI artwork. Original source sheets remain
unchanged. New seasonal sheets are opt-in through the saved Halloween setting.

## Painted accessories

`hollow-harvest-accessories.png`: front/side/back purple witch hats with velvet
folds, antique bone/gold buckles, violet crystal; carved pumpkin club; candle-lit
pumpkin cluster; prehistoric bone lantern. Prompt requested six independent
painted fantasy game accessories on a transparent 3-column/2-row atlas, matching
the amber/bone/purple prehistoric art direction. A second precise cutout pass
requested background removal while preserving the six objects and their edges.

## Creature sheets

`hollow-harvest-creatures.png`: precise Halloween skin edit of `characters-v2.png`.
Prompt preserved the 3x3 layout, humans and final meteor; skeletal purple-eyed
raptor, pumpkin-armored triceratops, spectral pterosaur, bandaged zombie sabretooth,
and pumpkin-carapace beetle replace only the ordinary monster artwork.

`hollow-harvest-monsters.png`: precise skin edit of `monsters-04.png`. Prompt
requested skeletal frilled raptor, pumpkin-plated armored dinosaur, purple mummy
mammoth, jack-o-lantern spider, spectral violet insect, bone thorn beast,
grave-rock golem, green ghost skeleton and purple/orange sail dinosaur. Original
positions, silhouette roles, direction and transparent backgrounds were retained.

`hollow-harvest-frontiers.png`: precise skin edit of `frontiers-07.png`. Prompt
preserved weapons/props and edited the middle creature row only: skeletal ice
sabretooth, haunted violet ammonite, jack-o-lantern armored beetle and pumpkin-cap
fungal spider. Renderer indexes their alpha bounds within original grid cells.

## Original soundtrack

`audio/hollow_0_0.wav` through `audio/hollow_2_2.wav`: nine original stereo
chip-fantasy loops composed and synthesized with `tools/build_hollow_harvest_music.py`.
Pulse/plucked leads, triangle-style bass, bell answers, original minor-mode
phrases and synthesized drums; 124–156 BPM with map/depth-specific melodies,
arrangements and instrumentation. No recordings, samples or copyrighted melodies
were copied. Loop metadata is in `audio/hollow-harvest-score.json`.
