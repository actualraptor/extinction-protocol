# 0.5 asset provenance

Existing original painted art is retained. Spell and frozen-creature presentation extends the authored effect atlases with native animation, faceted ice shading, lightning filaments and field motion. No third-party artwork was copied.

`tools/compose_audio.py` now renders 42 original stereo WAV assets: two music stems and 40 effects. New cues: thunderstorm, thunder_hit, chain_tick and venom_spit. Frost and electrical attacks were redesigned using deterministic noise, oscillators, envelopes, short reflections and soft saturation. Weapon unions reuse the appropriate original weapon sound family. No external audio samples or recordings were used.

The gameplay reference is the user-linked Vampire Survivors wiki, particularly its weapon union and evolution mechanics. All union names, recipes and implementation here are original.
