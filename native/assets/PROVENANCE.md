# Asset provenance

Created for this project using the built-in imagegen tool, September 29, 2026. No Megabonk or Vampire Survivors assets were used.

- `characters-v2.png`: active 3×3 transparent sprite atlas. Row 1: Mara, Kael, Vesper. Row 2: raptor, triceratops, pterodactyl. Row 3: saber-tooth, giant insect, meteor.
- `characters.png`: first draft retained for comparison; not referenced by the game.
- `terrain.png`: dark, tileable prehistoric ground texture.

Final sprite edit prompt: “Edit this 3x3 game sprite atlas to correct cell bleeding. Preserve all nine character identities, original painterly style, colors, weapons, pose and the exact row/column order. Scale down EACH INDIVIDUAL sprite into the central 70 percent of its own equal square cell, leaving a WIDE completely transparent padding gutter at all four sides of every cell. The nine equal square cells form precisely 3 columns and 3 rows over the entire image. No wing, tail, foot, weapon or particle may touch or cross its cell boundary. Remove every stray fragment outside each sprite. Absolutely transparent background, no floor/shadow or text, clean isolated full-body silhouettes. In particular ensure the insect legs and triceratops horns do not cross into the meteor or pterodactyl cells. This must be safely sliced into nine equal images without including any part of a neighbor.”

Terrain prompt: “A seamless square tileable texture for a premium hand-painted 2D top-down action game, prehistoric forest ground seen from directly overhead. Dark muted teal and olive soil, patches of worn moss, tiny ferns and thin exposed roots, occasional small slate pebbles, subtle organic detail. Very low contrast to ensure bright player sprites and attack telegraphs read over it. Flat ambient lighting, no perspective, no horizon, no big rocks, no trees, no text, no characters, no dramatic focal point. All four edges must seamlessly tile. Professional game background texture, painterly texture with crisp small details, earthy prehistoric ecology, square 1024px.”

Sound effects and music are synthesized in `scripts/sound.gd`. Terrain tinting, sprite movement, particles, lightning and other animation are generated in-engine. Godot's built-in font is used by the UI.
