# Extinction Protocol 0.14.1 — A World Reborn

## A Prehistoric World

Individually rebuilt dinosaur and boss artwork brings cleaner silhouettes, corrected anatomy and clearer creatures across all three maps. The opening story now matches the prehistoric roster.

## Survive The Stampede

Tightly packed Compy flocks rush across the battlefield, pushing survivors and nearby creatures aside. The first stampede arrives around 45 seconds into a run.

## A New Home For Discoveries

Browse a large discovery grid with categories, unlock states, requirements and a dedicated detail panel. Main-menu buttons, the official logo, moving mist and flickering lights give the menu a fresh look.

## Soundtrack And A Moment To Breathe

New orchestral themes accompany the maps and boss encounters, including a full score for the final countdown. Defeating a boss grants a brief respite from new spawns while the next horde gathers.

Existing progress is retained. The cinematic gallery contains only scenes you have unlocked.

This release replaces 0.14.0. The Windows download contains only player-facing files; internal test launchers are no longer bundled.
