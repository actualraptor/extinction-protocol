# 0.15.0 — Torr Stormbuk

Torr Stormbuk joins the Early Access expedition. Discover his unlock encounter in the world, then bring a divine arsenal into your next run.

- Eight [God] weapons with rank-5 and rank-10 paths and discoverable combinations. Ordinary survivors never draft God weapons.
- A detailed returning hammer: choose enemy-to-enemy ricochets or a permanent orbit. Additional projectile capacity adds hammers to the ring.
- Independently authored walking, throwing and catching poses; recorded character lines and a stormy illustrated first-time lesson with a useful replacement reward.
- An optional in-world challenge with a thunderous arrival, subtitles and a saved unlock. Completed illustrated stories are available in the Settings replay gallery.
- Includes the current weapon-path, combination, rarity-celebration and ornate run-scoreboard updates. Previous 3D boss reworks remain included.
- Fixed divine melee echoes, wide/narrow gauntlet fronts and dinosaur body hits. Tuned the divine melee arsenal and orbit path against late-game encounters.

This is an Early Access content release. Difficulty, animation transitions and heavy-crowd performance will continue to be tuned from player feedback. Automated boss damage-budget runs are not a substitute for human survival/playfeel testing.

Extract the ZIP and launch **Extinction Protocol.exe**. Existing saves and discoveries are retained. Sound is enabled for normal play. Private developer profiles and launchers are excluded.
