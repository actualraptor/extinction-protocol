# 0.11.2 provenance

Voss and Vesper starter attacks use original AI-generated artwork directed from this project's existing walk sheets and seasonal portraits. Four six-pose sources and 24 cleaned frames are retained in assets/hero-attacks-0111. Manual foot anchors preserve the existing 68px character scale. Complete generation and extraction notes accompany those assets. No third-party game art was copied.

Voss's 25 and Vesper's 23 MP3 recordings were supplied by the user. Audio was copied unchanged, with SHA-256 checks. Voss originals and runtime files now use voss_event_number.mp3; Vesper retains her previously standardized names. Source/runtime manifests record the mappings and hashes. Kael's existing 18 recordings remain unchanged.

Rarity cards reuse the project's slate art with stronger procedural metal borders, corner gems, animated highlights and tier crests. Patch art uses existing project cover art and game icons. Cinzel and Source Sans 3 font licenses accompany release archives.
