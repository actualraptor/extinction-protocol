# Extinction Protocol 0.14.2 — Dinosaur Boss Rework

## T-rex and Triceratops

The rebuilt T-rex and Triceratops are now in the public build. They use grounded pursuit, clearer spacing, directional attacks and creature sound to give the two finished bosses more presence in the arena.

## Meteor: first pass

Meteor has entered its first reconstructed presentation pass. Its encounter remains exceptionally hard and continues to receive updates to the entrance, shell motion, fire pressure and randomized wave patterns.

## Release notes

This build keeps the accepted dinosaur rework in the main game while Meteor continues through its next development passes. Existing progress is retained.
