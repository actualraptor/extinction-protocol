# Extinction Protocol 0.8.4 — A Connected HUD

- Rebuilt the bottom HUD as one continuous fossil-and-bronze dock.
- Weapons remain on the left, level and XP information in the center, resources and shortcuts on the right.
- Blue segmented XP now runs beneath all three groups without a plaque obscuring the fill.
- Removed the separate weapon and currency frames and oversized black background strip.
- Shared spacing and separators keep the dock readable across resolutions and HUD scales.
- Validation: 73 HUD checks passed across four resolutions and three HUD scales; rendered layout visually inspected.
