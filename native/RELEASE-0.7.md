# 0.7 — Frontiers

Three selectable expedition maps, five survivors, twenty-two base weapons, eighteen enemy species, and nine original map/biome combat compositions.

## Choose an expedition

- **The Lost Cradle**: the original jungle expedition, with Ashen Rift and Star Grave depths.
- **Frostbreak Expanse**: glacial shelves, ice avenues, Frostfang charging packs and Aurora Drifters. Opens after 250 cumulative expedition kills. Three depths: Ivory Shelf, Aurora Trenches, Frozen Firmament.
- **The Sunken Observatory**: connected astronomical courtyards, armored Meridian Scarabs and Spore Reliquary broods. Opens after your first expedition boss kill. Three depths: Drowned Court, Brass Meridian, Unmade Sky.

Each map has its own terrain layout, painted ground, local enemy composition, music, and first two boss patterns. Shared raptors and beetles keep the ecosystem connected. The final Extinction Engine encounter remains shared. The new maps have no hidden ground slowdown zones. Their full local rosters arrive after a gentler opening.

## Earn the arsenal

Every survivor always has access to their own starter kit. Existing unlocks and research are preserved. New milestones accumulate across expeditions and checkpoint during play; daily challenges and practice do not farm campaign progress. The Archive displays requirements and progress.

| Reward | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Mara | 100 cumulative kills, or the existing discovery/purchase route |
| Lost Ballistics | 500 cumulative kills, or discovery/purchase |
| Kael | 700 cumulative kills, or discovery/purchase |
| Riftcraft | 1,200 cumulative kills, or discovery/purchase |
| Iona + Fossil Harpoon | Find Iona's camp in Frostbreak's first biome |
| Orin + Hollow Lantern | Find Orin's camp in the Observatory's first biome |
| Glacier Wheel | 1,000 Frostbreak kills, or find its second-biome signal |
| Helios Repeater | 1,500 Observatory kills, or find its second-biome signal |

New camp and weapon discoveries unlock for free. Milestones grant automatically. Original exploration unlocks remain available. Historical kills from old versions are not guessed or backfilled; previously earned content stays unlocked.

Iona is a fast polar hunter with an ice damage bonus and piercing, knockback harpoons. Orin is an arcane specialist whose painted lantern wisps seek enemies and burst. Glacier Wheel ricochets and freezes; Helios Repeater fires rapid piercing solar bolts with burn damage. All four use the existing tag-aware upgrade system and have chest evolutions. The five-weapon limit remains.

## Music and artwork

Nine original 48-bar combat arrangements use layered pulse voices, arpeggios, bass, counter-melodies, percussion fills and contrasting sections. Every map/biome pair has a separate cue with crossfades. Solstice's ambitious NES sound is an inspiration; no melody, recording, or sample was copied. Four new weapon sounds accompany the new arsenal.

New generated assets include two directional character walk sheets, four illustrated weapon icons, four enemy illustrations, environment props, and six ground textures. New enemies use the existing GPU swarm rendering pipeline. Source compositions and asset indexing tools remain in native/tools.

## Design references

The progression structure draws on [Vampire Survivors achievements](https://vampire-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements), [weapon unlocks](https://vampire-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons), and the milestone/exploration structure described by the [unofficial Megabonk unlock guide](https://megabonk.org/guides/unlocks/). Names, art, maps, weapons, music and exact requirements here are original.

## Play

Run Play Extinction Protocol.cmd in the project, or extract the 0.7 Windows ZIP and run Extinction Protocol.exe. Choose a survivor, then an unlocked expedition. B opens stats; Tab shows the map. Old release folders remain available for rollback. Normal saves retain the same location and are migrated additively.
